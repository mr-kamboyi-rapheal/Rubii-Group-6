Who we are

We are Rubii (Group 6)

A team of Software Engineering students at our university who looked at a problem we face every single day — taking attendance — and asked why it still depends on paper. AttendQR is our answer: a simple, verifiable, digital attendance system designed for lecturers, students and administrators.

The problem

Manual attendance is slow, unreliable and easy to cheat.

In most lectures, attendance is still taken with a paper sheet passed around the room or a name-by-name roll call. It costs time and produces records nobody can fully trust.

Lost lecture time

Roll calls and circulating sheets can take 10–15 minutes of every class, especially in large groups.

Proxy signing

Students sign in for absent friends. Paper registers cannot tell who actually attended.

Lost & damaged records

Sheets get misplaced, torn or become unreadable, leaving gaps when records are needed.

Manual data entry

Lecturers retype names into spreadsheets — slow, repetitive work that introduces errors.

Who is affected?

  • Students lose learning time and can be wrongly marked absent.
  • Lecturers spend class time and admin hours managing registers.
  • Administration works with incomplete data when checking eligibility and reporting.

Why it needs to be addressed: attendance often decides exam eligibility. Records that are inaccurate or missing can unfairly affect students and weaken the institution's ability to monitor engagement.

Our proposed system

Meet AttendQR

A QR-based attendance management system that lets lecturers open a session in one tap and lets students check in from their own phones in seconds.

Purpose

Capture accurate, verified attendance quickly and store it digitally so it can be trusted and reported on.

Intended users

Lecturers who run sessions, students who check in, and administrators who review attendance records.

How it solves the problem

Time-limited codes stop sharing, student verification stops proxy signing, and digital storage ends lost sheets.

How it works

  1. 1

    Lecturer creates session

    Selects the course and opens a new attendance session.

  2. 2

    QR code generated

    A unique, time-limited code is shown on screen.

  3. 3

    Student scans

    Students scan the code with their phone camera.

  4. 4

    Attendance submitted

    Identity is verified and the check-in is sent.

  5. 5

    Record stored

    Saved with a timestamp, ready for reports.

Features

Everything needed for faster, fairer attendance.

AttendQR gives lecturers and students a simple way to run sessions, verify identities, and keep accurate records without the stress of paper registers.

Session creation

Lecturers can start a class session quickly and assign the correct course or group in a few clicks.

Live QR codes

Each session generates a unique QR code that stays valid only for a set time window to reduce cheating.

Identity verification

Student check-ins are matched to approved records so attendance reflects real participation rather than proxies.

Automated records

Attendance is stored with timestamps and can be reviewed, exported, or reported without manual re-entry.

Benefits

Why this system creates real value.

AttendQR helps institutions move from paperwork to dependable digital attendance records while making the process faster and easier for everyone involved.

Time-saving

Students check in quickly, and lecturers spend less time managing attendance during class.

Improved accuracy

Verified digital records reduce mistakes, make reports consistent, and support fair decision-making.

Better reporting

Administrative teams can review attendance data faster and identify trends without manual sorting.

Security & trust

Time-limited checks and verified logins reduce proxies and make records more credible.
